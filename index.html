01 / ABOUT

I build to learn.
I learn to build better.

I'm a frontend developer who spends most of his time turning ideas into real, working interfaces — not just mockups. My focus is modern UI, responsive layouts and JavaScript-driven interaction, with React where the project calls for it.

Motion is part of how I think about interfaces, not decoration added at the end — which is why GSAP shows up throughout this site, not just in a skills list. Lately I've also been exploring AI: how it fits into frontend workflows, and how it can become part of the products I build rather than a separate tool on the side.
